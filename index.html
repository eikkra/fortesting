Our Story

How It All Began

GOAT FC was born from a simple belief: competitive gaming should be built on respect, not ego. What began as a free-to-play eFootball community on 28 December 2023 has since grown into a structured digital football family with over a hundred members across Bangladesh.

We are a COBEG registered club that competes regularly in tournaments while maintaining a community where every player — regardless of device or division — is valued and supported.

Today, GOAT FC is not just about matches. It is about building character, sharing knowledge, and growing together as a generation of competitive eFootball athletes.

Code of Conduct

Core Values

🤝

Respect for All

Senior or junior, every member deserves equal respect. Toxic language or disrespectful behavior is strictly prohibited.

🎮

Skill Over Device

Gameplay varies by device and network. We never judge a player's ability based on their hardware.

🧠

Friendly Matches = Learning

Winning is not everything. Friendly matches are spaces to learn, improve, and enjoy the game together.

💬

Constructive Feedback

Advice belongs in private. Public criticism is not welcome. Approach teammates with empathy, always.

⚖️

Admin is Final

All disputes are resolved by the management team. The admin's decision is final and must be respected by all members.

🚫

Zero Tolerance

No political, offensive, or inappropriate content. A discipline system (warnings, suspension, removal) is in place for violations.
