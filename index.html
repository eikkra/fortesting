Origin

Who We Are

GOAT FC started on December 28, 2023 — born from a group of passionate eFootball players in Bangladesh who believed gaming communities should be more than casual lobbies.

We built a structured club with a rank system, friendly matches, and regular online tournaments. Every member gets an official Club ID card and a place in a real ranking ecosystem.

We also compete in Club vs Club (CVC) matches — taking on other eFootball communities to represent GOAT FC's name. Today we are 100+ active members strong, backed by a 16-member management team.

We don't just play matches. We build character, community, and competitive spirit — one game at a time.

Code of Conduct

Club Rules

01

Respect Everyone

Senior or junior — every member deserves equal respect. Toxic language, insults, or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.

02

Device Doesn't Define Skill

Gameplay varies by phone, device, and network. Never look down on a player based on their hardware or connection.

03

Friendlies Are for Learning

Winning isn't everything in friendly matches. Play to learn and enjoy. Avoid excessive clearing or purely negative play.

04

Keep Feedback Private

After matches, encourage your teammates. If you have criticism, take it to inbox — not the group. Public shaming is banned.

05

Admin Decision is Final

Any disputes go to the admin team. Their ruling is final and binding for all club members.

06

Discipline System

Rule violations result in yellow card (warning), red card (suspension), or permanent removal. No political or offensive content allowed.
