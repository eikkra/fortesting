Who We Are

More Than Just a Club

GOAT FC is a competitive yet community-driven eFootball club founded on 28 December 2023. Built with passion, discipline, and respect, we started as a free-to-play gaming community and have grown into a structured digital football family.

"To build a respectful, competitive, and skill-based eFootball family."

Today, we are more than just a club — we are a growing ecosystem of players, managers, and creators united by one vision.

Code of Conduct

Rules We Live By

We follow a strict but fair code of conduct. These aren't just rules — they're the values that define us.

  • 🤝 Respect everyone equally — senior or junior, winner or loser.
  • 🚫 Zero tolerance for toxic language, harassment, or disrespectful behavior.
  • 🎮 Device differences do not define skill. All platforms are equal.
  • 🧠 Friendly matches are for learning and growth, not ego or bragging.
  • 💬 Feedback must be constructive and private — not public criticism.
  • ⚖️ Admin decision is final in all disputes. Trust the process.
  • 🟨 Discipline system in effect: verbal warning → suspension → removal.
  • 🚫 No political, offensive, or inappropriate content in any GOAT FC space.

We are not just playing matches — we are building character.
